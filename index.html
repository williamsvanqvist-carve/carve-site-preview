Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Carve, in one place.

What is Carve?

Carve is a product-growth intelligence for Shopify brands. It investigates every product continuously, decides what will move it, and acts — with your judgment at the center.

How is Carve different from bulk catalog tools?

Carve is intelligence, not a pipeline. Bulk tools template one fix across the catalog; Carve investigates each product — performance, demand, the live page, competitors — and fixes the real constraint, whatever it is.

Can I keep my brand voice?

Yes — you define what good looks like, and Carve works in your brand’s voice and judgment, so every product still sounds and feels like you.

Where do Carve’s changes go?

Approved changes are written straight into your Shopify product pages, structured data and discovery surfaces, and stay live. When the real constraint is outside its reach — imagery, price, stock — Carve tells you instead.

Can I see what’s actually working?

Yes — Carve connects Search Console and analytics, ties every action to product impressions, clicks and organic revenue, and shows what it kept, revised or reversed.

How does Carve decide what to do?

It investigates first — building a truth set from your record, the live page, imagery, demand and competitors — then acts only on what the evidence supports. If something can’t be verified, it asks instead of inventing.

How easy is it to set up, and is it secure?

Carve connects to Shopify in minutes and works within your existing permissions — no exports, no spreadsheets, no extra security headaches.
