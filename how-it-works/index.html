How it works, plainly.

What Carve owns, what the manager controls and what happens when the evidence points elsewhere.

Ask William

No. The manager sets priorities, brand judgment and permissions. Carve gives that judgment catalog-wide reach by watching every product, doing the investigation and following through on approved work.

Carve can prepare or publish changes to supported Shopify fields, including titles, descriptions, metadata and Q&A. When the constraint sits elsewhere, it gives the manager evidence and a recommendation.

Carve tells you. If imagery, price, availability, assortment or another operational issue is the real bottleneck, the manager gets the evidence and recommendation instead of a cosmetic rewrite.

Rules can be set by field, product group and confidence. Carve can recommend, prepare a draft, request approval or act automatically. Every action keeps a before-and-after record.

Shopify provides the catalog and product surfaces. Search Console and GA4 connect demand, visibility and behavior. Carve also reads the live PDP and relevant market evidence when it investigates a product.

Each action stays tied to the products, evidence and performance that produced it. Carve can keep, expand, revise or reverse the work, then carry the lesson forward without blindly repeating it.
